Welcome to the brand new version of the Hackndo blog.

Previously hosted on a dedicated server, I was tired of paying every month for a server used only for the blog, and besides, I've never been very good at backups, so I figured a good solution would be to put everything on Github with their pages system.

<!--more-->

I have no regrets - it gives me exactly what I wanted, and I no longer have to worry about a disk failure or a flood that would jeopardize all my data.

Yes, I know, I could have set up a `cron` paired with a Hubic and all that, but I was also curious to discover Github's pages system as well as [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/){:target="blank"}, and I'm really happy with it.

I'm still on my trip around the world, but that doesn't stop me from learning about various topics. In the pipeline, a few articles on kernel exploitation, with quite a bit of theory summarized and made accessible, as well as practical articles because otherwise, it's not much fun.

Happy browsing everyone, stay tuned, I hope you'll like this new design.

See you around.
